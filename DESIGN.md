# HALO demo design

HALO is a reading surface for moments when a useful answer needs to arrive quickly and stay legible.

- Make the current sentence the largest object on the page.
- Expose the fast-to-clever handoff and its timing rather than claiming speed in decorative copy.
- Keep meeting selection and the question directly above the answer.
- Put full answers, evidence and note editing behind disclosure controls.
- Preserve source-backed retrieval, no-evidence behavior and sentence navigation.
- Keep the public demo honest: local retrieval and staged example answers, with no hosted model call.
